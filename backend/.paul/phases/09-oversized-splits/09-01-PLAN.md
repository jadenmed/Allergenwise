<objective>
## Goal
Reduce `app/(reviewer)/reviewer/queue/[submissionId]/page.tsx` from 990 lines to ~300 by extracting `AutoCheckRow`, `CertCell`, `DecisionDialog`, and a `useSubmissionDetail()` hook into co-located files, with zero behavior change.

## Purpose
Audit item B9 (Tier 3, pure clarity). File mixes fetch/state logic, two small presentational components, and three near-duplicate confirmation dialogs in one component — hard to review, easy to introduce a bug reviewers won't catch.

## Output
5 new co-located files + trimmed page.tsx. Same rendered markup, same network calls, same a11y attributes.
</objective>
