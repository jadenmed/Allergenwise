<objective>
## Goal
Migrate the 5 routes that currently call `renderEmail()` (from `lib/email/render.ts`) + `resend.emails.send()` by hand onto the canonical `sendEmail()` wrapper in `lib/email/send.ts`, so error handling, dual html/text rendering, and the `TemplateName` registry are single-sourced. Delete `lib/email/render.ts` once it has zero importers.

## Purpose
Audit item B3: two competing render paths exist today (`sendEmail()`'s internal render vs. the standalone `renderEmail()` helper). This is the safe half of B3 — every one of these 5 call sites already has a matching template registered or trivially registerable, so this is a pure plumbing swap with no new template content. (The harder half of B3 — sites needing brand-new template content — is 04-02.)

## Output
- `lib/email/send.ts`: adds `AccountDeletionConfirm` to `TemplateName`/`TemplateProps`/`buildEmail()`.
- 5 routes migrated off `renderEmail()` onto `sendEmail()`.
- `lib/email/render.ts` deleted (confirmed zero importers after migration).
</objective>
