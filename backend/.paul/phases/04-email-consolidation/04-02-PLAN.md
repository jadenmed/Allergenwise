<objective>
## Goal
Finish audit item B3 by creating the 3 templates that don't exist yet in `lib/email/send.ts`'s registry (an admin-facing exam-pass notice, a weekly admin digest, a reviewer-queue digest) and wiring the 4 remaining hand-rolled/manually-rendered call sites onto `sendEmail()`.

## Purpose
`exam/submit`, `stripe/webhook`, and the 2 cron digests are the last non-canonical email paths in the codebase. Unlike 04-01's sites, none of these can just swap in an existing template unchanged — `exam/submit` sends an ADMIN-facing notice that does not match the existing (unused) `ExamPassed` template's audience, and the 2 crons build ad hoc `<pre>` HTML with no template at all. Content must be created before routing can happen.

**Critical finding from research — do not reuse `ExamPassed` for exam/submit's admin notice.** `lib/email/templates/ExamPassed.tsx` is written for the LEARNER ("You passed, {recipientName}!", cert code framed as "your certificate"). The `exam/submit` route (line ~338) sends a DIFFERENT email to the restaurant's MANAGER ("Your team member has passed... their certificate will be issued when your restaurant admin completes payment"). These are two distinct emails for two distinct audiences. Sending `ExamPassed` to the manager would misaddress them as the exam-taker. `ExamPassed` itself stays unused/dead after this plan — wiring it up is out of scope (the learner-facing cert-pass email is deferred to the "CertActivated" P1 follow-up per the code comment at exam/submit.ts, not part of this audit item).

## Output
- 3 new template components + registry entries in `lib/email/send.ts` (union, props interface, `buildEmail()` case).
- `exam/submit/route.ts`: hand-rolled `<p>` HTML replaced by `sendEmail('EmployeeExamPassedNotice', ...)` with identical rendered content.
- `stripe/webhook/route.ts`'s `sendWelcomeAdminEmail()`: manual `render()` + dynamic imports replaced by `sendEmail('WelcomeAdmin', ...)`, adding the missing plain-text part as a side benefit (`sendEmail` always renders both).
- `cron/weekly-admin-digest/route.ts` and `cron/digest-reviewer-queue/route.ts`: hand-rolled `<pre>` HTML replaced by `sendEmail()` calls against the 2 new templates.
</objective>
